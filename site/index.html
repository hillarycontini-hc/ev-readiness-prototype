        Privacy

        • We ask for a ZIP code and four multiple-choice answers. Nothing else: no name, email, phone, or account.
        • Your ZIP is used in your browser to load public data for your area. It is never stored or sent to analytics.
        • We count anonymous page events (for example, “finished the questions”) with PostHog to improve the tool. Those events include your state and your multiple-choice answers, never your ZIP. No cookies, no session recording, no IP address kept.
        • If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track signal, we do not load analytics at all.

        An independent prototype by Hillary Contini, built on public data from NREL, EPA, the U.S. Census Bureau, EIA, and the National Park Service. Not affiliated with any automaker or dealer. Estimates only; not financial advice.
