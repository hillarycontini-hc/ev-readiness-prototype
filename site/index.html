              Privacy

              • We ask for a ZIP code and up to five multiple-choice answers. Nothing else: no name, email, phone, or account.
              • Your ZIP is used in your browser to load public data for your area. It is never stored or sent to analytics.
              • We count anonymous page events (for example, “finished the questions”) with PostHog to improve the tool. Those events include your state, your multiple-choice answers, your browser type, screen size, and the site that sent you here. Never your ZIP, and no location lookup.
              • No cookies, no session recording, and your IP address is not kept.
              • “Get my EV plan” asks for nothing: no email, no contact details. What you save in it (places, chargers, cars, trips) stays on this device unless you create a share link.
              • Addresses you search for in the plan go to our address service through our own server, so the service never sees you. To rank nearby results first, a search can include the rough location of your ZIP or a place you already picked. We don’t store any of it or send it to analytics.
              • The charger map is a street map of your area that our server fetches from our map service when you open the charging card. The page asks for a map on a fixed grid, the same picture for everyone nearby, and draws your ZIP center and the chargers itself. Your ZIP isn’t sent, but which map you see shows roughly where you are (in a city, often enough to guess the ZIP). Neither we nor the map service keep it.
              • When you plan a trip, its start (your saved home, the center of your ZIP, or a place you search) and its destination go to our routing service the same way, only when you press “Find charging stops”. They are not stored or sent to analytics.
              • If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track signal, we do not load analytics at all.
