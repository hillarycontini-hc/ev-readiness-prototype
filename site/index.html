              Privacy

              • We ask for a ZIP code and up to five multiple-choice answers. Nothing else: no name, email, phone, or account.
              • Your ZIP is used in your browser to load public data for your area. It is never stored or sent to analytics.
              • We count anonymous page events (for example, “finished the questions”) with PostHog to improve the tool. Those events include your state, your multiple-choice answers, your browser type, screen size, and the site that sent you here. Never your ZIP, and no location lookup.
              • No cookies, no session recording, and your IP address is not kept.
              • “Get my EV plan” asks for nothing: no email, no contact details. It just builds your checklist on the page.
              • If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track signal, we do not load analytics at all.

              An independent prototype by Hillary Contini, built on public data from NREL, EPA, the U.S. Census Bureau, EIA, and the National Park Service. Not affiliated with any automaker or dealer. Estimates only; not financial advice.
